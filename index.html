Living Heritage & Traditions

The Heart of Ethiopian Culture

Ethiopia is the birthplace of Coffee Arabica (*Buna*), teff grain superfood, and an ancient Ge'ez written script.

Origin of Coffee

The Traditional Coffee Ritual

Brewing coffee (*Buna*) in a clay pot (*Jebena*) over hot coals while frankincense burns is a foundational social ceremony of friendship, community, and hospitality across all regions of Ethiopia.

1

Abol (አቦል) - First Cup

The first and strongest brew, served with roasted popcorn or toasted barley (Kolo).

2

Tona (ቶና) - Second Cup

Fresh hot water is added to original grounds for a smoother, milder secondary flavor.

3

Baraka (በረካ) - The Blessing

The final cup imparting goodwill, health, and peace upon all family and visitors.

Injera & Beyaynetu

Spongy sourdough flatbread made from Teff superfood grain, served with colorful vegetarian stews (*Wat*).

Doro Wat

Rich chicken stew seasoned with Berbere spice mix, clarified spiced butter (*Niter Kibbeh*), and hard-boiled eggs.

Tej

Golden mead fermented from organic natural honey and Gesho leaves, served in flask-shaped *Berele* glasses.
